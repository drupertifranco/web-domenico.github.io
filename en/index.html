What I do

Configuring a device is the easy part. The hard part is keeping your network running just as well as it grows.

That's my work: I run, design and automate IP networks for ISPs and operators. BNG and PE, MPLS, BGP, OSPF, AAA/RADIUS. I've spent more than 4 years in telecom, and before this I worked in voice (Cisco, SIP) and radio (LTE/UMTS), so I see the whole network.

I work in two steps. First, operations done right. Then I move them into software: Containerlab labs to test before touching production, Python and Bash scripts, and LLMs connected to the network devices through MCP. Every problem I solve ends up as a tool that can be used again.
